## What this page solves

A calendar is not a list of dates. The real questions are: which race is the A race, which is practice, which ballot result changes the travel plan, and which clashes with recovery. Without those four layers, more entries mean more withdrawals and injuries.

This page treats published 2027 dates as the skeleton and unpublished dates as TBA. TBA does not mean “probably in month X”; it means “do not book yet”. You can use it for budget and training, not for a non-refundable flight.

## Core idea: A/B/C races and a ballot portfolio

The A race is the one race the year is built around: training, taper, travel and recovery all point to it. A B race is a practice race 8–12 weeks out, used to test kit and fuelling, not to chase a time. A C race is a local short race that keeps race feel without a long taper.

A ballot portfolio is not “enter more”. It combines different dates, distances and costs: one high-cost overseas ballot, one domestic race with a direct entry, one local fallback. Any hit can be executed; any miss does not empty the year.

## The 2027 skeleton: published and TBA

Published 2027 dates include Hong Kong 100 (23 January), Boston (19 April), Western States (26–27 June), Berlin (26 September), Chicago (10 October) and New York (7 November). These dates can anchor training, but they do not mean entry is open.

UTMB Finals, most Chinese races and several European trails remain TBA. A 2026 date is only a “usually around this time” reference, never a confirmed 2027 date. The calendar page keeps dated races first and TBA in its own column.

## Entry windows: separate ballots from direct entry

Western States 2027 lottery entry closed on 21 November 2026, with the draw on 5 December; if you read this now, prepare for 2028. Boston 2027 entry follows the current BAA notice, and a BQ is not acceptance. UTMB 2027 Finals dates are unpublished; the 2026 8–19 January window is only a rhythm reference.

Direct-entry races require a minute-accurate opening time, a pre-filled profile and a ready payment method. Ballot races require a screenshot of the submitted entry, not just an email refresh. The two failure modes are different.

## Travel, visas and budget

For an overseas race the order is: ballot/acceptance → payment → visa → flight → bed. Do not buy a non-refundable flight before the draw. Visa appointments can be slower than the ballot, especially for the US; put the interview before the entry deadline, not before the race.

Budget “entry + travel + bed + kit + insurance + 20% buffer”. An overseas A race usually costs 5–10 times the entry fee. If the budget only covers one, choose one that delivers qualification, experience and travel together rather than three half-prepared races.

## Recovery: the row most often deleted

Leave four weeks after an A race, two after a B and one after a C. Recovery is not “no running”; it is “no intensity and no travel”. Put recovery weeks in the calendar and make them as immovable as the race.

If two races are less than eight weeks apart, the second can only be B/C, never another A. Back-to-back A races look possible on paper and usually fail on immunity, sleep and tendons; build the calendar around recovery, not airfare.

## How to build the 12-month table

Place the A race first, then two B and three C races, then the recovery weeks. Allow only one high-stress event per month: an A/B race, an entry deadline, a visa interview, a move or a job change. Mark them red; if two months are red back-to-back, delete one.

Review quarterly: is training on plan, was the entry worth it, is travel over budget? The calendar is not a contract. Change B/C races, not the A race and not recovery.

## Common mistakes

Mistake one: treating TBA as “probably this month” and booking flights. Two: treating a BQ or qualifier as acceptance. Three: two A races in one month. Four: treating a ballot miss as training failure and entering another race immediately.

Five: comparing entry fees and ignoring travel and recovery. Six: leaving the visa to the end. Seven: using a 2026 Chinese race date as a 2027 plan. Each can waste a year.

## Case: an executable 2027 table

Example: Hong Kong 100 in January as B if selected; Boston in April as A if accepted; Western States in June as “lottery watch” with no training block; Berlin in September as B; Chicago in October as C; New York in November as an A fallback. Any miss is replaced by a local C race.

The table is not about the names; it is about four recovery weeks after every A, no time chase at B, and no long run at C. If you have one overseas budget, put the A on the race you most want and localise everything else.

## Checklist

Fourteen days before the deadline: confirm qualification/proof, passport validity, visa appointment, ballot/direct rules, refund and transfer terms, refundable travel and the recovery week. Forty-eight hours before: log in again and confirm payment and email.

Eight weeks before the race: check B/C races do not clash. Four weeks out: confirm A-race travel and taper. Two weeks out: read the official guide only; stop changing the plan.
